Income elasticity of demand

YED = %ΔQ ÷ %ΔY. Income elasticity of demand (YED) tells you how responsive consumers are to income changes. Their responsiveness varies between whether the product is a normal good, an inferior good or a luxury good. Choose a product from the list to see the different impact of a change in income.
