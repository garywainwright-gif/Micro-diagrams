Subsidies

A subsidy is a payment from the government to producers for each unit they sell. It lowers their costs, so supply shifts right. Press Elastic or Inelastic to set the slope of demand, then press a word to highlight that part of the diagram.

S is supply before the subsidy. S1 is supply after the subsidy. D is demand. P and Q are the original equilibrium.

Press Elastic or Inelastic, then press a word. The supply shift stays the same. The demand curve changes, and so does each highlight.
