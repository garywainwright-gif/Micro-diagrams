An indirect tax is a tax on spending. The firm pays the government, so the firm’s costs rise.

£2 Tax introducedThe tax is £2 on every unit. Supply curve shifts. Price moves up the Demand curve.
Two pricesBuyers pay more than sellers receive. The gap between those prices is the tax.
Who pays the £2Inelastic demand: buyers pay more of it. Elastic demand: sellers pay more of it.
