Consumer and Producer surplus

Back to interactive games

Consumer surplus is the extra benefit consumers get because they pay less than the maximum they were willing to pay. Producer surplus is the extra benefit producers get because the market price is higher than the minimum they were willing to accept. Choose either consumer actions or producer actions to see how consumer / producer surplus changes.
